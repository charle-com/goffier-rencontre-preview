Four expressions of the material

For this first presentation, Maison Goffier unveils four interpretations of its work with pâte de verre.

  • Bleu Glacier

    Transparency, depth and light.

  • Bleu Profond

    A dense material and a depth of colour that give light its full intensity.

  • Stries blanches

    A graphic language in which striations run through the material and structure the light.

  • Alvéolaire bleu / rouge

    A more expressive composition in which reliefs, colours and transparencies make each piece singular.

A unique piece, by nature

Pâte de verre never repeats itself exactly.

Its nuances, reliefs, transparencies and the reactions of the material give each creation its own character.

Maison Goffier makes this singularity the heart of its work.

Each piece is unique.

It can be made to order or give rise to a bespoke creation, conceived for a place, an architecture or a particular project.

Dimensions, colours, glass nuances, metal finishes and lampshades can thus be the subject of a specific study.

The excellence of glass, the magic of light.
